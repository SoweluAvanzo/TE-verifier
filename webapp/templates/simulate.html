Run a Monte Carlo simulation against the failure-mode safety predicates. The verifier tells you whether each cliff is reachable; this page tells you how often you walk off it.

How this complements the verifier
  • SOUND failure modes are skipped — the verifier already proved they cannot trigger.
  • BROKEN failure modes are skipped with a notice — no parameter assignment in the declared box can satisfy them, so likelihood is uninformative.
  • FRAGILE / INCONCLUSIVE failure modes are simulated. For each, the page reports P(deployment-time violation) vs P(dynamic violation) — a configuration problem vs a drift problem — plus the distribution of time-to- violation.
  • The Export cadCAD config button emits the full simulation contract as JSON: initial state, PSUBs per simulable FM (with safety predicates verbatim), and the sweepable-parameter grid. Drop it into your own cadCAD pipeline.
