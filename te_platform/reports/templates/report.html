The mathematical check (worst case). Each question corresponds to a condition that a sustainable design must meet, taken from published research on why token economies fail. A computer program searches all combinations of values within your ranges for one that breaks the condition. If there is none, the answer “holds in every case” is a proof, not an estimate; if there is one, the question “can fail”, and the report says whether adjusting your numbers would be enough.

The typical case. The same conditions evaluated with every value at the middle of its range: how the design behaves under ordinary conditions. A question can fail in the worst case and still be fine in the typical case; that means it fails only in less likely combinations of your values.

The simulation. For the questions the mathematical check could not settle for every case, the community was re-enacted on the computer member by member, week by week, many times, with values drawn at random within your ranges, to see how often the problem actually appears and how soon.
